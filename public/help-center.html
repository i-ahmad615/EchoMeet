Help Center

Get practical support quickly

Use these quick guides to troubleshoot common workflows in EchoMeet.

Account access

Use Login/Signup from the top navigation. If your dashboard looks out of date, sign out and sign back in to refresh your account identity.

Friend requests

Search users on the dashboard, send requests, and use Incoming/Sent request controls. Refresh after actions to confirm persistence.

Call setup

Allow camera and microphone permissions. Start calls from Your Friends and keep one account per browser profile.

Assistive features

Open chat/features panel during calls for voice-to-text, text-to-voice, sign workflows, and AI responses.
