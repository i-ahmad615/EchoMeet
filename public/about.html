About EchoMeet

Our mission is to make communication truly accessible.

EchoMeet is built for people who need communication to adapt to them, not the other way around. We combine video calls, assistive tools, and AI guidance into one professional experience.

Our story

EchoMeet started with a simple observation: many calling tools assume one communication style. Real people communicate through spoken words, typed messages, signs, and context-driven support. We built EchoMeet to bridge those modes without adding friction.

Today, our product vision focuses on reliable, respectful, and inclusive conversations for education, support teams, families, and community organizations.

Our values

  • Inclusion first: every feature should support more people, not fewer.
  • Clarity over complexity: simple workflows that reduce communication stress.
  • Trust and privacy: responsible handling of user data and clear controls.
  • Continuous improvement: we refine based on real communication needs.

Product vision

Become the most dependable communication hub for mixed-mode conversations at personal and organizational scale.

Accessibility commitment

We maintain readable contrast, keyboard-friendly interactions, and adaptable layouts as core standards, not optional add-ons.

Responsible AI

AI in EchoMeet supports users with translation and helpful responses while keeping user control central.
